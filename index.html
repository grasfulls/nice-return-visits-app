Install Nice Return Visits

NRV is designed to be used from its Home Screen icon. This browser page is only the doorway to installation; it cannot be used to work with NRV contacts.

We’ll guide you through adding the NRV icon. After you open that icon, NRV will check for previous NRV information and continue setup or offer recovery choices.

Use normal Safari, not Private Browsing. NRV cannot reliably identify Safari Private mode. If this page is in a Private tab, stop here, close that tab, and reopen NRV in a normal Safari tab. Private use is unsupported, and NRV cannot recover information created or lost in a Private session.

Scroll down for instructions to install NRV to your Home Screen.

  1. Tap the three horizontal dots in the bottom-right corner.
  2. Tap View More.
  3. Tap Safari’s Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  4. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  5. Tap Add.
  6. After adding, this screen may minimize.
  7. Swipe up to bring this screen back.
  8. Scroll down and tap I added NRV to my Home Screen.
  9. Close this window, then tap the new NRV icon on your Home Screen.

After Add to Home Screen, return to the icon—not to Contacts in Safari. Setup or restoration continues when NRV opens from the icon.

Installation page complete

This part of installation is complete. Close this Safari tab or window. Find the NRV icon on your Home Screen and tap it to continue installation and setup.

Cities, Regions & Areas

Add New City to List

How locations work: A city is divided into regions, which are large parts of the city (such as North, South, East, and West). A region is divided into areas, which are smaller neighborhoods within that region. Cities, regions, and areas are added as you enter them for yourself or a contact; use each field's + Add New choice to add one.

Add New Region

You can also add a region while adding a contact. Choose + Add New Region... in that contact's Region list to add one for the selected City. A region name can be used in more than one city; the city you choose makes it unique.

Add New Area

Manage Cities, Regions, and Areas

Cities, regions and areas are each listed once. Edit renames a region or area everywhere and lets you choose which cities it is assigned to. Delete is blocked while any contact uses it; deleting a city also removes its region and area assignments, after you confirm. Regions and areas are independent: a contact can have a city, a region, an area, or any combination.

Data/Security
Passphrase to Open App

Passphrase

Turn Off Passphrase?

If you turn the passphrase off:

  • The contacts and settings on this device are decrypted and stored without encryption. Anyone who can open this browser or app on this device could read them.
  • The app opens right away and no longer asks for a passphrase.
  • Your recovery code stops working, because there is no passphrase left to recover.
  • Backups you make from now on, including Dropbox backups, are not encrypted. Backups you already made keep the passphrase they were created with.
  • You can turn a passphrase on again at any time.

Recovery Code

A recovery code is a long random code you keep somewhere safe, away from this device. If you forget your passphrase, type the code in place of the passphrase to open the app. Anyone with the code can open your data, so treat it like the passphrase.

Backup or Restore

Backups protect you from losing your contacts.

One device: Local backup is normally sufficient. Multiple devices: use Dropbox when you want NRV to coordinate them automatically. Keep a separate recovery copy somewhere NRV cannot manage.

Local Backup/Restore

Encrypted if a passphrase is on. You can also export from the Contacts page footer.

A downloaded Local backup survives Erase All Data. For a protected recovery copy, keep a separate copy outside NRV’s working storage.

Cloud Backup/Restore (Dropbox)

Cloud backup keeps a copy of your contacts outside this device, in your own Dropbox, so you can recover them if your device is lost, broken or replaced. It is off unless you turn it on. Dropbox is entirely optional: the app works fully without it, and all your data can stay only on this device.

Dropbox is the active backup and synchronization location. To preserve a protected recovery copy, use Dropbox’s own tools to copy or move a backup outside NRV’s Return Visits app folder. NRV cannot see or change that separate copy; download it later to restore it as a Local backup.

You need a Dropbox account. The app can send you to Dropbox to sign up, but it cannot create the account for you. The free Basic plan is enough.

Do you already have a Dropbox account?

Create a free Dropbox account

  1. Open dropbox.com/basic (it opens in a new tab or window).
  2. Sign up with your email address (or your Google or Apple account) and choose a password.
  3. Choose the free Basic plan. If Dropbox offers a paid trial, skip it. No payment is needed.
  4. Check your email and confirm your address if Dropbox asks.
  5. Come back to this app and tap the button below.

Connect your Dropbox

  1. Tap Connect to Dropbox below.
  2. Sign in to Dropbox when it asks.
  3. Tap Allow. The app can only use its own folder, called Return Visits, and cannot see anything else in your Dropbox.
  4. Dropbox sends you back here.

The app backs up when you open it if your last backup is more than a day old, and shortly after you make changes. Dropbox keeps your latest copy plus up to 10 dated copies.

Using more than one device? Each device combines its changes with Dropbox automatically: when you open the app, every couple of minutes while it is open, and as soon as the connection returns after being offline. Visits and contacts added on different devices are all kept. If you have no signal, your changes stay saved on the device and upload as soon as it can reach Dropbox. The cloud icon at the top shows the state (tap it to sync now). You can also tap Check for a Newer Backup.

If two devices change the very same contact at the same time, the most recent change to that contact is used, but visits from both devices are kept.

Erase All Data / Start Fresh

Before erasing, create or verify a current backup and preserve a separate recovery copy outside NRV’s normal storage or Dropbox app folder. Erasing removes this copy’s contacts, settings, passphrase/security data, setup choices, and local Dropbox connection. Downloaded backup files and Dropbox files are not deleted. Removing the Home Screen icon alone does not necessarily erase NRV data.
